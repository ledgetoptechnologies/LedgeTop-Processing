# Synthetic v2 direct chunk spool

This disabled fixture-only direct-call spool builds on the authenticated held v2 geometry registry. It uses a separate v2 slot/journal namespace, a durable coordinator creation phase before allocation, real `posix_fallocate`-checked same-inode storage, and ordered chunk intent, data sync, verified receipt and full-archive seal. It revalidates the claim, result metadata, geometry and slot identity; ambiguous or missing phases and markers hold. Current v1 readers reject v2 markers, including a remaining slot row after geometry loss. There is no automatic cleanup.

A small synthetic archive was actually allocated, transferred through direct calls, replayed and sealed; an actual child SIGKILL after writing a chunk left an intent without a receipt and replay held. A separate test wrote 4,096 one-byte chunks and 8,194 journal records, with about 39 MiB peak Python RSS and no directory enumeration after header creation. A 1.5-GiB value was checked only by geometry arithmetic; no >1-GiB file was reserved or processed, and sparse files are not considered capacity proof.

A separate fixture-only TLS route and opt-in promotion/Viewer reader exist, but there is no production wire negotiation, Viewer custody proof, or physical fence against older processes. It has no production activation; old binaries may ignore its markers. A call accepted before a hold can still finish, and the v2 seal alone authorizes neither Viewer access nor deletion. Additional versioned claim/transport integration, old-writer exclusion, allocation and large-byte testing, crash qualification and independent review are required.
